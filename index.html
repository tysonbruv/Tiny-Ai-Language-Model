A QUICK HELLO

Big ideas. Tiny models.

This playground makes a few language-model ideas easier to see.

  1. Start with some text.A small sample is already loaded. You can use your own.
  2. Try a prompt.Generate a result, then open “See how it worked” if you’re curious.
  3. Move to the next stage.Each stage adds a new idea. Compare runs the same prompt through all five.
These are teaching simulations.

The five stages use word chains, sentence matching and formatting rules. They are not real GPT models, and do not implement neural-network training or attention. The model numbers are a learning ladder, not a measure of real model performance.

Use it as an app

Once hosted over HTTPS, this app can work offline after its first visit. On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Other supported browsers may show an Install app button.
